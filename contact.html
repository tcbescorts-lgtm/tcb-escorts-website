A pilot (escort) car is a vehicle that travels ahead of, behind, or alongside an oversize or overweight load. It warns other drivers, checks clearances and route conditions, and helps the load move safely and legally to its destination.

Requirements vary by state and depend on a load's width, height, length, and weight. Most states require at least one escort once a load exceeds roughly 12 feet wide, with additional escorts and high pole cars required as dimensions increase.
