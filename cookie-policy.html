Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies (like browser local storage) are actually used on tcbescorts.com, based on a direct review of this Site's code. We have written this to describe what the Site does, not a generic template.

1. Analytics Cookies Are Optional

The Site works without analytics. The first time you visit, a banner asks whether to allow Google Analytics. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you click Accept. If you click Decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, it is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer. We do not run Meta/Facebook Pixel or advertising trackers.

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-G45DSEGPS2) sets the following first-party cookies, which help Google count visits and distinguish visitors:

_ga — identifies a browser to distinguish users.
_ga_G45DSEGPS2 — stores session state for our Analytics property.

Google Analytics receives page views and events such as clicks on call, text and email links and successful form submissions. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization.

2. Local Storage (Not a Cookie)

The Site uses your browser's local storage for two things: your language preference (key tcb-lang) and your analytics choice (key tcb-analytics-consent). These values stay on your device, are not transmitted to us, and contain no personal information. Clearing your browser's site data resets them, and the banner will ask again.

3. Cookies Set by Embedded Third-Party Services

Certain pages embed content from Google, which may set its own cookies or similar identifiers that we do not control:

Google Maps — embedded on the Home and Contact pages to display our Jackson, MS location. Loading the map may allow Google to set cookies and process data under Google's own privacy policy.
Google Fonts — used to load this Site's typefaces (Oswald and Inter) from Google's servers. This involves a direct connection from your browser to Google, which may log your IP address as part of normal web request handling.

We do not receive any data from these Google-hosted resources ourselves; the connection is directly between your browser and Google.

4. Form Submissions

Our Quote Request and Driver Application forms submit directly to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which may use its own cookies or session identifiers as part of processing the submission and preventing spam/abuse. This only occurs at the moment you submit a form.

5. Outbound Links

The Site links to our Instagram profile. That link only takes effect if you click it; visiting instagram.com is subject to Meta's own cookie and privacy practices, not ours.

6. Managing Cookies and Local Storage

Because the Site does not need cookies for its own functionality, you can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time without breaking the Site's core functionality. Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their privacy or settings menu.

7. Changes

If we add other analytics, advertising or tracking technology, we will update this Cookie Policy first and ask for your consent before it loads where required.
