Pilot Car Services for Oversize Loads

Lead, chase, high pole and steer escorts, plus route surveys, matched to your load, your route and your permit. Based in Jackson, Mississippi, serving 48 states.

Escort Position

Lead Pilot Car

The lead pilot car drives ahead of the load and looks at the road before the load gets there. It watches for low clearances, tight turns, work zones, stopped vehicles and oncoming traffic, and radios the driver in time to react. Many permits call for one at the front of the move.

  • Scouts the route ahead of the load
  • Two-way radio contact with the load driver
  • Warns oncoming traffic and helps at intersections where permitted

Escort Position

Chase Pilot Car

The chase pilot car follows behind the load. It keeps other vehicles from crowding the trailer, helps the load change lanes and merge, and watches the load itself for shifting cargo, loose tie-downs or tire and light problems. On wide loads the rear escort is often what keeps traffic out of the lane the load needs.

  • Rear traffic awareness and lane protection
  • Watches the load and trailer while moving
  • Warning lights, flags and signs as the state requires

Escort Position

High Pole Pilot Car

When a load is tall, a high pole car leads the move with a height pole set a few inches above the load's tallest point. If the pole touches a wire, sign or bridge, the team stops before the load reaches it. Whether a high pole is required is usually spelled out on the permit.

  • Pole set and checked against the load's height
  • Checks overhead clearance ahead of the load
  • Used with tall loads under wires, signs and bridges

Escort Position

Steer Escort

Steering support helps long or articulated loads through tight turns and intersections, where the trailer can swing wide or track off the road. The role and the credentials it requires differ from state to state, so tell us about your trailer and we will confirm what your move needs.

  • Support through tight turns and narrow roads
  • Coordinates with the load driver and other escorts
  • Requirements for the role vary by state

Pre-Trip Service

Route Survey

A route survey is a drive-through of the planned route before moving day. We check clearances, turns, narrow stretches and trouble spots so surprises show up on paper instead of on the road. It pairs well with the permit review your carrier already does.

  • Clearance and obstruction check along the route
  • Turns, intersections and pull-off areas noted
  • Findings shared before your load moves
