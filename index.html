What We Do

Escort Services

We scope every job to your route, permits, and dimensions, from a single steer car to a full multi-vehicle escort package.

Lead Escort

Travels ahead of the load to identify hazards, low clearances, and traffic conditions before the truck arrives.

Learn More →

Chase Escort

Follows behind the load to manage trailing traffic and protect the rear of the movement.

Learn More →

High Pole Escort

Equipped with a height pole to detect overhead obstructions ahead of tall loads.

Learn More →

Steer Escort

Guides the load through tight turns and congested areas where extra maneuvering support is critical.

Learn More →

Route Survey

Pre-trip route analysis to confirm clearances, permits, and turn radii before moving day.
