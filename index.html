Our Services

Escort Services Built Around Your Load

From a single steer car to a full multi-vehicle escort package, we scope every job to your route, permits, and dimensions.

Lead Escort

Travels ahead of the load to identify hazards, low clearances, traffic changes, and road conditions before the truck arrives.

Learn More →

Chase Escort

Follows behind the load to manage trailing traffic, provide visibility to other drivers, and protect the rear of the movement.

Learn More →

High Pole Escort

Equipped with a height pole to detect overhead obstructions — bridges, wires, and signage — ahead of tall loads.

Learn More →

Steer Escort

Guides the load through tight turns, intersections, and congested areas where extra maneuvering support is critical.

Learn More →

Route Survey

Pre-trip route analysis to confirm clearances, permits, and turn radii, reducing delays and surprises on moving day.

Learn More →
