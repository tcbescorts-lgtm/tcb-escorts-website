What a Pilot Car Does

Escort vehicles that keep oversize loads moving safely

An oversize load is harder to steer, stop and merge than an ordinary truck. A pilot car travels with it to scout the route, warn other drivers and keep the driver informed by radio. We run lead, chase, high pole and steer positions, plus route surveys when a move calls for one.

Every move starts with your permit and route, because the state and the load decide how many escorts you need. Send us the details and we will help you confirm the right setup.

Escort Positions

Pilot Car Services

We scope every job to your route, permit and load dimensions, from a single escort to a full multi-vehicle package.

Lead Escort

Drives ahead of the load, watching for low clearances, tight turns, work zones and oncoming traffic, and radios what it sees.

Request this service →

Chase Escort

Follows behind to keep traffic from crowding the load, help with lane changes and merges, and keep an eye on the load itself.

Request this service →

High Pole Escort

Leads with a height pole set just above the tallest point of the load to check clearance under wires, signs and bridges.

Request this service →

Steer Escort

Steering support for long or articulated loads through tight turns and intersections, as the assignment requires.

Request this service →

Route Survey

A drive-through of the planned route before moving day to check clearances, turns and trouble spots.
