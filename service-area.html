Pilot Car Services Across 48 States

Mississippi is our home base. From Jackson, our dispatch team arranges pilot car and escort vehicle coverage along your route across our 48-state service area.

Service Area

Home base in Mississippi, coverage well beyond it

Many of our moves start or end in Mississippi and the Southeast, but loads rarely stay in one state. We do not currently serve Alaska or Hawaii. Tell us where you are picking up and delivering, and dispatch will line up escorts along the full route using our own vehicles and our established network of drivers.

  • Pilot car and escort services across the 48 contiguous states
  • Attention to each state's certification and permit conditions
  • Short-notice requests and multi-state routes
