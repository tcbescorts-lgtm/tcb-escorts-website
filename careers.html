Requirements

What You'll Need to Join

We hold every partner in our network to the same standard we hold ourselves. No documents on file means no dispatch, so have these ready when you apply.

  • Valid driver's license
  • Current escort/pilot car certification for your state(s)
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI)
  • Completed W-9 form
  • Proper signage and equipment (high pole equipment if applicable)
