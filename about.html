Our Story

Years of Experience, One Standard That Never Changes

TCB Escorts LLC was built by people who understand what's at stake when an oversize load hits the road: tight clearances, tighter schedules, and zero room for error. We've escorted more than 1,300 loads for over 110 carrier and broker partners across the country.

What hasn't changed is our approach: certified drivers, honest communication, and a dispatch team that treats every load like it's the only one on the road that day.

Our Standards

Every Escort Is Vetted Before They're Ever Dispatched

Whether it's one of our own vehicles or a partner in our nationwide network, no one runs a TCB-dispatched load without clearing our standards first.

Verified Licensing & Insurance

Valid driver's license, current escort certification, and proof of insurance on file before dispatch, no exceptions.

Written Confirmations

Every assignment is documented (route, schedule, load specs, and escort type) before a driver hits the road.

Active Job Monitoring

We confirm pilots are on-site before pickup, track progress, and proactively flag any delays to the client.

Certifications

Credentials Our Drivers Carry

Documentation is available on request for any client or state agency that needs to verify it.

WITPAC Certified

Nationally recognized pilot/escort vehicle operator training covering safety procedures, signage, and multi-state regulations.

TWIC Card

Transportation Worker Identification Credential, required for access to secure port and freight facilities.

State Certifications

State-specific escort vehicle operator certification requirements for oversize load movements, including New York.

NSC Defensive Driving

National Safety Council Defensive Driving Course certification for accident prevention and safe operation.

Certified Pilot Car Operators (CPC)

Formal certification confirming our operators meet industry training and competency standards for pilot car work.

PEVO Certified

Pilot/Escort Vehicle Operator certification, recognized across multiple states for oversize load escort work.

Fully Insured Escort Services

Every escort operates under active commercial insurance coverage, verified before dispatch, no exceptions.
