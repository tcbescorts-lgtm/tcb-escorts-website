About TCB Escorts

TCB Escorts LLC is a pilot car company based in Jackson, Mississippi, providing lead, chase, high pole and steer escorts and route surveys for oversize and overweight loads across 48 states.

Who We Are

A Mississippi pilot car company that works across 48 states

Oversize moves leave little room for error: tight clearances, fixed permit windows and drivers who need accurate information. TCB Escorts exists to supply that information and the escort vehicles that go with it, so the load and the people around it get where they are going safely.

We work with heavy haul carriers, freight brokers and owner-operators. To date we have escorted more than 1,300 loads for over 110 carrier and broker partners, and our dispatch team is reachable by phone or text.

Our home base is Jackson, Mississippi, and we cover 48 states through our own vehicles and our established network of escort drivers.

How We Work

What you can expect on every assignment

Drivers checked before dispatch

We confirm a valid driver's license, current escort certification for the states involved and proof of insurance before a driver is assigned.

Details in writing

Route, schedule, load specifications and escort positions are confirmed with you before anyone leaves for the pickup.

Contact while the load moves

We confirm escorts are on site before pickup, follow progress and tell you early if something changes.

Credentials & Insurance

$1 Million Insured, with credentials on request

TCB Escorts carries $1,000,000 in insurance coverage. We keep certificates, policy records and driver documents private, and send them directly to carriers, brokers or agencies that need to verify them.

Request Insurance & Credentials

Credentials our drivers hold

  • WITPAC: pilot/escort vehicle operator training
  • TWIC: Transportation Worker Identification Credential, for secure port and freight facilities
  • State certifications: state-specific escort operator credentials, including New York
  • NSC Defensive Driving: National Safety Council course
  • CPC: Certified Pilot Car credential
  • PEVO: Pilot/Escort Vehicle Operator certification

Not every credential applies in every state. Requirements depend on where your load travels.
